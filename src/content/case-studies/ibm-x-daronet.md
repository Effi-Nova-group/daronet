## The Task

IBM Think brings together business leaders, technology decision-makers, innovators, developers, partners, and enterprise customers to explore the technologies shaping the future of business.

For IBM, the opportunity extended far beyond attracting attendees to the conference. The objective was to understand the audience at a deeper level—what they cared about, which technologies generated the strongest interest, what content resonated with them, and how those insights could be transformed into better conference experiences, stronger enterprise sales opportunities, and higher-performing marketing campaigns.

IBM used **Daronet** as an AI-powered audience intelligence and marketing platform to bring these elements together.

Through deep audience analysis, Daronet helped IBM identify the interests, behaviours, conversations, preferences, and emerging themes surrounding the IBM Think audience. Those insights were then transformed into actionable recommendations across conference content, enterprise lead generation, organic social media, paid advertising, creative production, and media buying.

The result was a highly integrated, audience-led marketing strategy.

**Conference audience engagement reached 78%—the highest level recorded.**

IBM's social media advertising campaigns generated an **11x ROAS**, also representing its highest-performing social media campaign result.

Following the success of the initiative, IBM expanded its relationship with Daronet, becoming a **global partner and user of the platform for its own activities and client campaigns worldwide.**

## The Challenge

A major global conference generates enormous amounts of audience information.

Attendees search for topics before the event. They engage with speakers and content online. They discuss technologies across social channels. They interact with sessions, products, demonstrations, advertisements, videos, and thought leadership.

The challenge is turning all of those signals into useful intelligence.

IBM wanted to move beyond traditional conference analytics and understand the **people behind the numbers**.

The key questions included:

- **Who is attending IBM Think?**

- **What technologies, products, and business challenges are most important to them?**

- **What topics are generating the strongest engagement?**

- **Which content should IBM prioritise during the conference?**

- **Which attendees and audience segments represent potential enterprise sales opportunities?**

- **Which IBM products are most relevant to those audiences?**

- **How can those insights improve organic and paid social media performance?**

Daronet provided IBM with a central intelligence layer designed to answer those questions and convert the findings directly into marketing and commercial actions.

## The Approach

### 1. Deep Audience Intelligence

IBM used Daronet to conduct an in-depth analysis of the IBM Think audience.

Rather than treating conference attendees as a single demographic group, Daronet analysed the audience across multiple dimensions to identify distinct interests, behaviours, conversations, affinities, and areas of technology demand.

Daronet's AI reporting consolidated these signals into actionable audience intelligence.

IBM could identify which subjects were attracting the greatest attention and understand the differences between individual audience segments.

Instead of asking:

"What content do we think the audience wants?"

IBM could begin with:

"What is the audience already telling us it wants?"

That distinction became fundamental to the IBM Think strategy.

### 2. Building Conference Content Around Audience Demand

Daronet's audience intelligence was shared with the teams responsible for IBM Think programming and content.

The insights highlighted the topics, technologies, challenges, and areas of innovation generating the greatest audience interest.

IBM used this intelligence to help optimise:

- Conference themes

- Presentations

- Sessions and workshops

- Product demonstrations

- Thought-leadership content

- Social content

- Speaker messaging

- Supporting digital content

Content could therefore be aligned more closely with what the audience actually wanted to learn about.

#### Result: 78% Audience Engagement

**The audience-led approach contributed to an engagement level of 78%—the highest recorded for the conference.**

Rather than using audience intelligence only to promote IBM Think, IBM used it to improve the experience **inside the event itself**.

### 3. Turning Conference Intelligence Into Enterprise Sales Opportunities

The value of the audience intelligence did not end when the conference finished.

Daronet's analysis was also provided to IBM's enterprise sales organisation.

The reports helped identify audience segments demonstrating strong interest in specific technologies, business solutions, and IBM products.

**This created an important bridge between marketing intelligence and sales intelligence.**

Instead of handing the sales organisation a generic database of conference attendees, IBM could provide greater context around audience interests and potential commercial intent.

Enterprise sales teams could use these insights to:

- Identify potential leads

- Prioritise audience segments

- Understand the interests of prospective customers

- Match audience needs with relevant IBM solutions

- Personalise sales conversations

- Determine which products to promote

- Develop more relevant follow-up communications

The conference audience therefore became more than an event database.

It became an actionable enterprise opportunity.

### 4. Audience Intelligence Powered IBM's Social Strategy

IBM also used Daronet's AI reports to shape its social media strategy.

Audience intelligence identified the topics and conversations generating the strongest interest, allowing IBM's marketing teams to develop organic content around demonstrated audience demand.

Instead of creating content first and attempting to find an audience afterward, the process was reversed:

**Audience → Intelligence → Content → Distribution → Optimisation**

Organic social content was developed around the subjects the audience was already engaging with.

This helped create a more relevant content ecosystem before, during, and after IBM Think.

### 5. AI-Powered Advertising Production

Daronet was also used to transform audience intelligence directly into advertising.

Using insights from its AI reports, IBM developed campaign concepts and creative designed around the interests of individual audience segments.

Daronet supported the production of the complete advertising ecosystem, including:

- Static advertising creatives

- Video advertising concepts and assets

- Campaign messaging

- Primary text and introductions

- Headlines

- Descriptions

- Calls to action

- Audience-specific messaging

- Product-focused advertising

- Creative variations for testing

**This connected audience discovery and creative production within the same marketing workflow.**

The people creating the advertising were therefore working from the same intelligence being used to understand the audience.

### 6. From Audience Insight to Media Buying

Daronet's audience analysis also informed the structure of IBM's paid social media campaigns.

Rather than separating audience research, creative development, messaging, and media buying into isolated activities, IBM could connect them.

#### The Daronet workflow:

1. **Analyse the Audience**

2. **Identify Interests & Intent**

3. **Create Audience Segments**

4. **Identify Relevant IBM Products**

5. **Generate AI Marketing Recommendations**

6. **Develop Organic Content**

7. **Generate Static & Video Advertising**

8. **Create Headlines, Intros & Descriptions**

9. **Build Paid Social Campaigns**

10. **Launch, Measure & Optimise**

This created a closed-loop marketing process in which audience intelligence influenced almost every major campaign decision.
