## The Task

GNC wanted to better understand the rapidly growing consumer interest around **longevity, healthy aging, performance, and nutritional supplements**—and turn that intelligence into measurable e-commerce growth.

Rather than relying only on traditional demographic targeting or historical campaign data, GNC used **Daronet** to conduct a deep analysis of audiences demonstrating interest in longevity supplements and related wellness categories.

The objective went beyond advertising.

**GNC wanted to understand what consumers were interested in, what topics were gaining momentum, what products they were looking for, what conversations were trending, and what messages were most likely to generate engagement and purchase intent.**

Daronet transformed this audience intelligence into a complete strategy spanning product offering, paid media, creative development, organic social content, and global e-commerce marketing.

The result: **8x ROAS from the global GNC e-commerce campaign.**

## The Challenge

The longevity and wellness market is evolving rapidly.

Consumers are increasingly researching topics around healthy aging, energy, cognitive performance, recovery, sleep, metabolic health, nutrition, and long-term wellbeing.

For GNC, the challenge was not simply identifying people interested in supplements.

**The challenge was understanding what those consumers actually cared about—and how those interests were changing.**

GNC needed deeper intelligence to answer critical questions:

- Which longevity and wellness topics were generating the strongest audience interest?

- What consumer needs and product categories were gaining momentum?

- Which trends were becoming highly discussed or “hyped” across digital channels?

- What messaging would resonate with different audience segments?

- Which products should be highlighted to each audience?

- What creative concepts were most likely to drive engagement and conversions?

- How could the same intelligence improve both paid advertising and organic social media?

Daronet provided the intelligence layer connecting these questions to actionable marketing decisions.

## The Approach

### 1. Deep Audience Analysis

Daronet analyzed audiences demonstrating interest in longevity supplements and associated health and wellness topics.

Instead of treating this audience as a single consumer group, Daronet identified different interests, behaviors, conversations, trends, and potential purchase motivations within the broader longevity market.

The analysis helped GNC understand not only **who the audience was**, but also:

- What they were interested in.

- What they were talking about.

- What was trending.

- What products were relevant to them.

- What content captured their attention.

- And what messaging was most likely to move them toward purchase.

This intelligence created a much more detailed picture of the potential GNC customer.

### 2. Audience Intelligence Influenced Product Offering

One of the most valuable applications of the Daronet analysis extended beyond media buying.

GNC used the audience insights to help improve its **product offering and product positioning** around the interests identified within the longevity audience.

Rather than building the marketing strategy independently from consumer demand, audience intelligence helped inform which products and product categories should receive greater visibility.

This created a powerful feedback loop:

**Audience Interest → Consumer Insight → Product Offering → Marketing → Sales**

The objective was to create greater alignment between what consumers were actively interested in and what GNC presented to those consumers through its e-commerce experience and marketing.

### 3. Worldwide GNC E-Commerce Campaign

Daronet then translated the audience intelligence into a worldwide campaign promoting **GNC e-commerce**.

Audience insights became the foundation of the media strategy.

Instead of delivering generic supplement advertising across every market and audience, Daronet used its audience analysis to align campaign messaging and creative with specific consumer interests.

Different audiences could therefore receive advertising designed around the themes and motivations most relevant to them.

### 4. AI-Powered Creative Matching

The audience analysis also informed the campaign's complete creative strategy.

Daronet used audience intelligence to help determine the most relevant:

- Static images

- Video concepts

- Primary copy

- Ad introductions

- Headlines

- Descriptions

- Messaging angles

- Product positioning

- Calls to action

Creative was therefore developed around audience intelligence rather than assumptions.

For example, an audience showing stronger interest in healthy aging could receive a different message from an audience focused on performance, energy, recovery, cognitive wellbeing, or other wellness goals.

The goal was simple:

**Right audience. Right product. Right message. Right creative.**

At scale.

### 5. Turning Audience Trends Into Organic Content

GNC also used **Daronet AI Reports** beyond paid advertising.

The reports identified emerging audience interests, conversations, trends, and areas of growing hype within the longevity and wellness market.

GNC used these insights to help develop its **organic social media strategy**.

Instead of planning social content solely around a fixed publishing calendar, teams could identify subjects currently capturing consumer attention and develop relevant content around those conversations.

Daronet AI Reports therefore helped inform:

- Social media topics

- Educational content

- Product-focused posts

- Trend-driven content

- Consumer-interest stories

- Emerging wellness conversations

- Organic creative concepts

This enabled GNC to respond faster to changing consumer interests and connect its products with conversations already taking place in the market.

### One Intelligence Layer Across Paid + Organic

A major advantage of the Daronet approach was that the same audience intelligence could be applied across multiple marketing functions.

**Audience Analysis → Product Intelligence → Creative Strategy → Paid Media → Organic Social → E-Commerce**

Rather than operating these activities independently, GNC could use a shared understanding of the consumer across the entire marketing ecosystem.

Paid campaigns revealed opportunities. Audience intelligence informed content. Organic engagement provided additional market signals. Those signals could then contribute to future campaign and creative decisions.

This created a continuously evolving marketing cycle driven by real audience behavior.
